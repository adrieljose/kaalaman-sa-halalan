Privacy Notice

Effective: 21 September 2026

Kaalaman sa Halalan is operated by Keinth L. Horario as a COMELEC-affiliated educational project. Questions about privacy may be sent to keinthhorario@gmail.com.

Information used by the game

The game can store your optional certificate name, completed chapters and difficulty levels, current checkpoint, certificate status, and tutorial status. It does not require an account and does not ask for payment details, contact lists, precise location, or sensitive personal information.

Why and where it is stored

This information is used only to restore game progress and create an in-game certificate. It is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. It is not uploaded by the game to a player account or database.

Hosting information

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like other web hosts, Vercel may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, approximate location derived from IP, browser/device information, request logs, and usage or diagnostic data. See Vercel’s Privacy Notice.

Cookies, analytics, and third-party embeds

The game currently uses no cookies, advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or third-party embeds. If this changes, this notice and any required consent controls will be updated before those tools are enabled.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains in the browser until you clear this site’s browser data or replace it through the game’s save/load features. You may remove it through your browser’s site-data settings. Hosting logs are retained according to Vercel’s policies and applicable law.

Your rights

Subject to the Philippine Data Privacy Act and applicable exceptions, you may ask about access, correction, objection, deletion, or other rights concerning personal information controlled by the operator. Contact keinthhorario@gmail.com. For concerns about Vercel-hosted log data, consult Vercel’s notice as well.

Children

The game is educational and does not knowingly collect children’s information through an online account. A certificate name is optional and stays in the browser. A parent, guardian, or teacher should supervise younger users and may clear the site’s stored data at any time.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the game’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
